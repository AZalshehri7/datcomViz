Body base % radius
Rolls the 90° base edge into a fillet so the mesher meets a curve rather than a corner. Applied to the written file only.
Most components come out as closed solids, but they are not fused to each other — a fin sits on the body, not joined to it — so the model as a whole is not watertight. A boolean union in CAD or a mesher is well posed. Three exceptions are open or non-manifold on their own: mirrored lifting surfaces meet at the centreline, jet nacelles are uncapped tubes, and stacked LUG/SHOE protuberance members overlap. Facet normals follow right-hand winding throughout. Neither format records units, so the deck's DIM goes in the filename and the header. Deflections are written at the angles in the boxes above, at full scale — the Deflection slider only affects the view.
